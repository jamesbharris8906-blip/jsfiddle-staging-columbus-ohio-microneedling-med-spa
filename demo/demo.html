Questions People Ask

How long will my visit last?

Plan for about an hour for most visits. A first visit may take a little more time, and the team will let you know when you book so you can plan around it.

Is it painful?

Most people feel only mild soreness for a short time. The team works gently and will pause if you need a break, so you stay in charge of the pace.

What will I pay?

You will get a clear price after your first check, since each plan is different. Ask for the full cost in writing, and ask about payment choices too.

How fast do results show?

Some changes are quick, and some build over days or weeks. Your team will tell you what to look for and when your next check should be.

How often do I need to come in?

It depends on your goals. Some people visit each month, and some visit a few times a year. Your team will help you pick a pace you can keep.

Do I have to book in advance?

It is best to book ahead, but call to ask about open spots. The team keeps a list of times that free up, and they will fit you in when they can.

What should I have with me on day one?

Have your medicine list, past notes, and questions ready. Bring ID and your payment card, and wear clothes that make the visit easy.

How to Get Ready for Your Visit

A little prep can make your visit feel calm from start to end. The night before, put your ID, your payment card, and your list of questions in one spot by the door. Write down the top three things you want from the visit, and bring that paper with you. If you feel nervous, tell the front desk when you arrive. They hear it all the time, and they can give you a few extra minutes to settle in. Eat a light meal, drink water, and arrive a few minutes early so you can fill in forms without a rush. After you book, save the office number in your phone, so you can call with ease if plans change. A calm, ready visit is a good visit, and it starts with these small steps.
